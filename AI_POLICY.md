# AI contribution policy

AI assistance is allowed for code, tests, documentation, research, and review. Contributors remain responsible for the accuracy, security, maintainability, and licensing of what they submit. Review and test AI-assisted code to the same standard as any other contribution.

Do not add AI-generated creative assets to this repository or the app. This includes images, logos, icons, illustrations, textures, mockups, video, voices, music, sound effects, and similar visual or audio material. The rule applies regardless of file format, including assets represented as SVG or other code.

Use original human-created assets or appropriately licensed human-created third-party assets, and keep the source and license information needed to use them. AI may help write code that implements the interface; that permission does not extend to generating the creative assets displayed or played by the interface.
